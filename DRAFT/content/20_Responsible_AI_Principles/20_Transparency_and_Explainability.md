## Transparency and Explainability
| Maturity Level | Defining Requirements                                                                 | Operationalization & Communication                                                            |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **1 - Basic**   | Transparency and explainability goals are unclear or discussed on an ad hoc basis.    | AI outputs are presented without context or user-facing explanations.                         |
| **2 - Intermediate** | Requirements for explainability are defined for relevant use cases and stakeholders. | Explanation tools or methods (e.g., SHAP, LIME) are applied to selected models.               |
| **3 - Advanced** | Stakeholder-specific transparency and explanation standards are formalized and documented. | Explanations are systematically generated, validated for effectiveness, and tailored to end-users. |
